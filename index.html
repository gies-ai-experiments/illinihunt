IlliniHunt — discover what the University of Illinois has built

A launch board for the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Students, faculty, and staff post the apps, startups, research tools, and side projects they have built; the campus community upvotes and comments on them.

Browsing is open to everyone. Submitting a project, voting, and commenting require signing in with an @illinois.edu account through the university's Microsoft Entra ID tenant. Projects are filed under eight categories — Business & Entrepreneurship, Creative & Entertainment, Emerging Technology, Health & Wellness, Learning & Education Tools, Productivity & Organization, Research & Data Analysis, and Social & Communication.

The home page lists projects newest first. The trending page ranks them by a time-decay score, so recent momentum beats old vote totals.
